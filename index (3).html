What the tagline means in practice.

Freedom

A written plan sets your trading hours, the markets you watch and the most you will risk in a day. Once the plan is done you can step away from the screen.

Discipline

You fill in a checklist before every trade and review the result afterwards, whether you won or lost. Your mentor reads the journal.

Currency

We cover forex pairs, gold and the major stock indices: how they move, what moves them, and how to size a position so that a single loss stays small.

The 12-week programme

You learn to limit your losses before you learn to look for entries.

Weeks 1–2

Market basics

How currency pairs, pips, lots and trading sessions work, and how to read a price chart.

Weeks 3–4

Risk first

Position sizing and stop-losses, and why we risk one percent or less of the account on any single trade.

Weeks 5–6

Finding an edge

Choose one setup, write down its rules and test them on past charts before using them.

Weeks 7–8

The trading mind

Fear, revenge trades and overtrading, and the routines that stop them.

Weeks 9–10

Journal and review

Log every trade, then go through the journal with your mentor each week.

Weeks 11–12

Live practice

Trade your written plan on a demo account while mentors give feedback during the live sessions.
